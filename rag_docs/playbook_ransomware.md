# PB-RANS-01 Ransomware

Owner: Incident Response. Severity guidance and steps below are mandatory for this alert category.

## Applies to
Shadow copy deletion, backup catalog deletion, mass file renames with new extensions, ransom notes.

## Response steps
- Isolate affected servers; the network team may shut down SMB at the core switch.
- Verify immutable backups in the offsite vault.
- Do not power off encrypted hosts (preserve memory).
- Notify the CISO, legal, and the cyber-insurance carrier within 1 hour.

## Additional guidance
Do not contact or pay the threat actor; all communication goes through legal and the insurance carrier.
