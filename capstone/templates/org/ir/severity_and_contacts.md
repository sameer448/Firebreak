# IR-POLICY-01 Severity and Escalation Policy

Owner: Incident Response. Plain-language version of the severity levels and escalation rules in org.json, so the IR agent and analysts can read them. Keep it in sync with org.json.

## Severity
SEV1: score 7 or more, Tier-0 involved, or destructive impact. SEV2: score 5 to 6. SEV3: score 3 to 4. SEV4: below 3. Score = asset criticality + impact + scope.

## Escalation
Paging a human and contacting anyone outside security always require approval by the SOC shift lead. Cases, war-room channels and summaries are created automatically.
