# HVH-IR-POLICY-01 Severity and Escalation

Owner: Incident Response. Plain-language version of the policy in org.json.

## Severity
Score = asset criticality + impact + scope. SEV1: 6 or more, Tier-0, or destructive impact. SEV2: 4 to 5. SEV3: 2 to 3. SEV4: below 2. EHR, PACS and pharmacy systems are critical assets; nurse stations and clinical workstations are high.

## Escalation
IR on-call is paged for SEV1-SEV2; the clinical-systems on-call and the CMO are added for SEV1. The HIPAA Privacy Officer is engaged for any suspected exfiltration. Every page and every stakeholder or regulator contact requires approval by the SOC shift lead.
